## Decision Making

Senior engineers become valuable because they:
- choose tradeoffs
- handle ambiguity
- decide quickly with incomplete information
### Improve this by:
When learning anything, ask:

- Why this approach?
- What are alternatives?
- What are tradeoffs?
- What breaks at scale?
- What would Netflix/Uber do differently?


## Psychology & People Skills

- Emotional control
- Observation
- Listening
- Calmness
- Persuasion
### Learn these:
#### A. How people think
Most people:
- want respect
- hate embarrassment
- respond emotionally first, logically second
Understanding this changes teamwork completely.
#### B. Conflict Handling

In companies:
- ego exists
- politics exists
- miscommunication exists

Never become:
- overly emotional
- reactive
- defensive

The calm person usually wins.
#### C. Negotiation
- Ask questions first
- Understand the other side
- Never negotiate emotionally
